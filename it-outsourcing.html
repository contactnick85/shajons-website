Technology

IT Outsourcing by Shajons.

Technology should be the quietest part of running a business. When it is not, people lose hours to faults, data sits at risk, and growth slows.

Shajons provides IT outsourcing in two connected parts. Managed IT keeps your people, devices, networks and data secure and available. Software and digital services build the websites, applications and business systems you run on.

We work with small businesses that need dependable support at a sensible scale, and with larger organisations that need a structured, accountable technology partner.

What you get
  • A service desk and engineers aligned to your hours and sites
  • Security, backup and recovery built in from the start
  • Websites and software built to your process, not a template
  • ERP that fits a ten-person team or a multi-entity group
  • Tablyvio restaurant POS with setup, training and support

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Do you support small businesses as well as large companies?

Yes. Engagements are scaled to the size of the business, from a small team needing remote support to multi-site organisations needing a dedicated managed team.

Can you build a website and also look after it?

Yes. We design, build, host and maintain websites, and we can take over and improve an existing site.

Which ERP do you implement?

We recommend a platform based on your processes, budget and growth plans, then configure, migrate data, train users and support the system after go-live.

Is my data safe with an outsourced team?

Access is role-based and logged, staff are vetted, and confidentiality terms are part of every agreement.
