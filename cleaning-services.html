Cleaning Services by Shajons.

Cleanliness is how most people judge a building within seconds of walking in. It also protects health, preserves finishes and reflects directly on the organization inside.

Shajons provides cleaning as a supervised, measured service. Each site has a defined scope, a task schedule, trained staff, approved materials and equipment, and regular inspection against agreed standards.

We cover the full soft-services range: routine cleaning, specialist and deep cleaning, high-level and façade work, waste, pest control and washroom hygiene, so one provider can look after the whole building.

What you get
  • Trained, uniformed and supervised cleaning teams
  • A written scope and task schedule for every area
  • Machines, tools and approved chemicals supplied
  • Regular quality inspections with scorecards
  • Flexible shifts including early, evening and night cover

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Do you supply equipment and consumables?

Yes. Machines, tools and approved cleaning chemicals are included. Washroom consumables can be supplied and replenished under the same agreement.

Can you provide cleaning outside business hours?

Yes. Early-morning, evening and night shifts are common, so that cleaning does not disturb occupants.

How do you control quality?

Supervisors inspect against written checklists, scores are recorded, and results are reviewed with you regularly.

Can you handle one-off or project cleaning?

Yes. Post-construction, move-in, event and deep-clean projects can be booked without a long-term contract.
