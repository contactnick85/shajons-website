Questions

Tablyvio questions answered.

Does Tablyvio work without internet?

Yes. The installable version has an offline mode that keeps orders, payments and receipts going, and syncs when the connection returns.

Which countries is it set up for?

Tablyvio is configured for the country you trade in, covering currency, tax rate, tax-inclusive or added pricing, tax numbers on receipts and credit notes for refunds.

What devices and printers do I need?

Windows, Mac, Android, iPhone and iPad in Chrome, Edge or Safari, plus an installable app for Windows and Mac. Any 80 mm thermal receipt printer works.

How can customers pay?

Through card terminals such as Stripe Terminal, Pine Labs and Razorpay POS, payment links from your own Razorpay or Stripe account, and UPI QR codes in India.

What does Shajons do for a restaurant adopting Tablyvio?

We help with the kick-off call, menu setup, hardware advice, staff training and go-live support, as part of our IT Outsourcing service.

How do I find out about pricing?
