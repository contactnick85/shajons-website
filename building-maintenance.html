Building Maintenance by Shajons.

A building is only as good as the systems inside it. Cooling, power, water, fire protection and vertical transport must work every day, and the cost of neglect in a climate like Dubai's arrives quickly.

Shajons delivers building maintenance as a managed service. We survey the property, register its assets, build a planned preventive maintenance calendar, and staff the site with qualified technicians supported by a central helpdesk.

Where specialist OEM or regulated work is required, such as lifts and fire systems, we coordinate authorised contractors and keep the records in one place, so you deal with one accountable partner.

What you get
  • A dedicated site team or visiting technicians sized to the property
  • Planned preventive maintenance with a published schedule
  • A helpdesk for requests, faults and emergencies
  • Asset register, work-order tracking and monthly reporting
  • Spare-parts and contractor management under one agreement

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Can you take over from an existing maintenance contractor?

Yes. We run a structured handover that includes an asset and condition survey, so there are no gaps in cover during the transition.

Do you maintain lifts and fire systems yourselves?

We manage and coordinate these through authorised specialist partners, as required by regulation, and keep all inspection records in your single reporting pack.

Can we start with only one discipline?

Absolutely. Many clients begin with HVAC or electrical maintenance and extend the scope once the relationship is established.

How is performance measured?

Through service levels agreed in the contract: response and completion times, preventive maintenance completion, and repeat-fault rates, reported monthly.
