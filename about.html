About Shajons Facility Management

A Dubai-based facility management and business services company, founded on the belief that the best service is the kind you rarely have to think about.

Who we are

One partner for the buildings, systems and people behind your organization.

Shajons Facility Management serves corporate, hospitality, healthcare, education and residential clients in Dubai and across the UAE.

We combine the technical trades that keep a building running with the people, technology and advisory services that keep an organization running inside it. Building maintenance, cleaning, IT outsourcing, recruitment and education and career advisory sit under one management framework, so clients can replace several suppliers with one accountable partner.

Our approach is simple: understand the premises and the people who use them, agree measurable standards, staff each site with vetted and supervised teams, and report openly on results.

Our founder

Three decades of operating experience.

J
JohnFounder

Shajons is founded and led by John, a veteran of business operations with more than thirty years of working experience across the Middle East and India.

Over those decades he has worked in the operational side of the industries Shajons serves: the services that keep buildings running, the people who deliver them, the technology behind them and the advice organizations rely on. That depth means he understands what clients need from a service partner, and what it takes to deliver it day after day.

His experience in two regions also shapes how Shajons works. Standards of service, labor markets, supplier networks and client expectations differ between the Gulf and India, and understanding both gives the company a practical advantage when planning teams, sourcing talent and managing suppliers.

John remains closely involved with clients and operations, and sets the principles every Shajons team works to: accountability, discretion, consistency and partnership.

Our team

Strong people in every field we serve.

Shajons is organized as five specialist teams under one management framework. Each is built around people who are skilled in their own discipline, supervised by people who have done the work themselves.

Technical & maintenance team

Trade-qualified technicians and supervisors who diagnose properly and fix things once.

  • HVAC, electrical, plumbing and fire-safety trades
  • Planned preventive maintenance and fast reactive response
  • Supervisors who inspect, record and report

Cleaning & hygiene team

Trained crews working to written method statements and checklists.

  • Daily, deep, façade and specialist cleaning
  • Correct chemicals, equipment and safety practice
  • Site supervisors with inspection routines

Technology team

Engineers, developers and designers who build and support the systems you rely on.

  • Web, software and ERP delivery
  • Service-desk and managed IT support
  • Mobile apps, integrations and ongoing support

Education advisors

Counselors who know the schools, universities and pathways they recommend.

  • School selection and admissions guidance
  • University, study-abroad and career pathways
  • Institutional advisory and parent support

Recruitment team

Recruiters who understand frontline, technical and managerial roles from the inside.

  • Sourcing, screening and verification
  • Bulk, trade and executive hiring
  • Onboarding, documentation and follow-up
