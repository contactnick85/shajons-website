Talent

Talent Acquisition by Shajons.

The right hire changes a team, and the wrong one costs months. Companies across the Gulf need a talent partner who understands the roles, the labor market and the regulations in each country.

Shajons Talent Acquisition works as an extension of your HR team. You tell us the roles, headcount and timeline. We search our candidate network, screen and verify applicants, present shortlists, and handle offers, documentation and onboarding.

Because we also run facility, cleaning and IT operations, we understand frontline and technical roles from the inside. We can supply a single specialist, a full project crew, or an entire managed workforce.

What you get
  • Pre-screened candidates matched to the role and your culture
  • Speed for urgent and high-volume requirements
  • Coverage across industries from facility services to executive leadership
  • Onboarding, documentation and visa support handled for you
  • Optional management of outsourced staff, including payroll
  • One recruiter who knows your business

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What kinds of companies do you supply talent to?

Employers across facility services, hospitality, retail, construction, healthcare, education, logistics, technology and corporate sectors, from small businesses to large groups.

Can you manage staff on our behalf?

Yes. Under an outsourced-manpower agreement we employ, supervise and pay the team while they work at your site.

How quickly can you fill urgent roles?

Timelines depend on the role, but we maintain a pre-screened pool for common technical and frontline positions so that urgent requirements can be met quickly.

Can you hire for roles outside the UAE?

We are based in Dubai and support employers across the UAE and the wider Middle East. Tell us the country and the roles and we will confirm how we can help.

Do you handle visa and documentation?

We prepare and coordinate the documentation and processes needed through the relevant authorities and approved service channels.

Speak to us about talent acquisition.

Tell us about your property, organization or requirement. A senior member of our team will respond to arrange a consultation or site visit.
