Recruitment Services by Shajons.

The right hire changes a team. The wrong one costs months. In a market as fast-moving as the UAE, employers need a recruitment partner who understands both the talent and the regulations.

Shajons Recruitment Services covers the full spectrum: permanent placements, temporary and contract staff, executive search, bulk and trade hiring, and fully outsourced manpower for facility, hospitality and corporate clients.

Because we also run facility, cleaning and IT services, we understand frontline and technical roles from the inside, and we can supply and manage whole teams, not just CVs.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Can you manage staff on our behalf?

Yes. Under an outsourced-manpower agreement we employ, supervise and pay the team while they work at your site.

How quickly can you fill urgent roles?

Timelines depend on the role, but we maintain a pre-screened pool for common technical and frontline positions so that urgent requirements can be met quickly.

Do you handle visa and documentation?

We prepare and coordinate the documentation and processes needed through the relevant authorities and approved service channels.
